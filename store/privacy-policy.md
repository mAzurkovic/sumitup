# Sum it up! privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Sum it up! is a Chrome extension that summarizes web pages and selected text with an AI provider you choose, using your own API key. This policy explains what the extension handles and where it goes.

## What goes to your AI provider

Page content goes to the AI provider you select. Today that provider is Anthropic.

The extension sends content only when you click:

- **Page summary:** the main text of the page in the tab where you opened the panel.
- **Selected text:** the text you selected, when you click "Sum it up!" or More.
- **Chat:** your question, the earlier messages of that chat, and the page or selected text it is about.

Nothing is sent when a page loads, when you navigate, or when you select text without clicking.

Each request goes straight from your browser to the provider, with your API key. Anthropic handles that content under its own terms and privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

## What stays on your device

- **Your API key, model, and detail level** are kept in Chrome's extension storage on this device. Page scripts cannot read them. Your key is sent only to your provider, with each request. You can delete it at any time from the panel's model menu.
- **Summaries, chat, and page text** for each tab are kept in Chrome's session storage until the tab closes or Chrome quits.

## What we do not do

- There is no Sum it up! server. The developer receives none of your data.
- There are no accounts, analytics, tracking, or ads.
- No data is sold or shared with anyone other than the provider you select.
